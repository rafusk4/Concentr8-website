Caffeine alone spikes, then drops. Caffeine with theanine holds.

That pairing is the whole idea. Low sugar means almost nothing to crash from, the vitamins and salts stop the lift thinning out, and the last two are in there for the third hour rather than the first twenty minutes.

High caffeine content. Not recommended for children or for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Concentr8 is a food, not a medicine, and the recipe itself stays protected.
