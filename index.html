Caffeine alone spikes, then drops. Natural caffeine holds.

The other seven earn their place the same way the can earns its look. Nothing is on there that isn't doing something. Green tea softens the lift, the vitamins and salts stop it thinning out, and ginseng is in for the third hour rather than the first twenty minutes.

High caffeine content. Not recommended for children or for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Concentr8 is a food, not a medicine, and the recipe itself stays protected.
